## User journeys / Clinical trial recruitment

[P-01] Service commitment 1
  Disclose unknowns affecting delivery or price; no implementation source is accessible to tender respondents.

[P-02] Service commitment 2
  The commissioned platform must feel instantaneous to users; the performance workload and latency threshold remain unstated.

[P-03] Service commitment 3
  An external regulator accepts solely the incumbent nightly batch file, which remains a live dependency.

[P-04] Service commitment 4
  Procure a capability to coordinate consented recruitment for research trials, currently supported by desktop clients and an embedded database.

[P-05] Service commitment 5
  The clinical trial recruitment work package includes verifiable screening logs.

[P-06] Service commitment 6
  Privileged override actions bypass the audit trail and require no logged justification.

[P-07] Service commitment 7
  Custom source deliverables become buyer property.

[P-08] Service commitment 8
  The personal data processor must isolate environments and log individually attributable access.

[P-09] Service commitment 9
  Deliver protocol eligibility, with a walkthrough using realistic clinical trial recruitment records.

[P-10] Service commitment 10
  Erase personal case histories at the end of month 6.

[P-11] Service commitment 11
  Demonstrate participant withdrawal against operational clinical trial recruitment scenarios.

[P-12] Service commitment 12
  Financial acceptance requires exact calculation parity with incumbent rounding and historical adjustments.

[P-13] Service commitment 13
  Offline credential caching is prohibited for authentication.

[P-14] Service commitment 14
  Include holiday on-call staffing under the personnel schedule, with no dedicated rate or service boundary.

[P-15] Service commitment 15
  Active interface consumers are unknown; the purchasing body has no verified integration catalogue.

[P-16] Service commitment 16
  The procurement terms offers no exit assistance, source escrow or usable data-export commitment if the appointed provider fails.

[P-17] Service commitment 17
  Interface boundary: protocol eligibility; maintain continuity for clinical trial recruitment.

[P-18] Service commitment 18
  A footnote to invoicing calls for suppliers to print and post statutory notices to residents each month.

[P-19] Service commitment 19
  Digital delivery mandates accessibility verification under EN 301 549 prior to sign-off.

[P-20] Service commitment 20
  Backups are copied daily, but the agreement calls for no restore test or recovery evidence.


## Operational promises / screening logs

[A-01] Service commitment 1
  Include hardware refresh for every obsolete endpoint, an obligation appearing exclusively in the facilities schedule.

[A-02] Service commitment 2
  Identity infrastructure is outside scope, but the supplier must replace the enterprise directory as part of delivery.

[A-03] Service commitment 3
  Retain every personal case history for seven years; anonymisation is not permitted.

[A-04] Service commitment 4
  Price the migration of all historical records; the commissioning body supplies no inventory, volume or size estimate.

[A-05] Service commitment 5
  The licence annex says ownership of custom source never leaves the vendor.

[A-06] Service commitment 6
  Mobile teams must authenticate new users at disconnected locations.

[A-07] Service commitment 7
  Recovery exercises must demonstrate a four-hour restoration target and a one-hour data-loss limit.

[A-08] Service commitment 8
  The proposed solution must export structured records using documented open formats, with an annual portability test.

[A-09] Service commitment 9
  Every release must pass regression tests in a distinct non-production environment in advance of approval.
