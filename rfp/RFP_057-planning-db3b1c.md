# Service design charter: Public consultation submissions

Reference: 057-planning-db3b1c
Primary outcome: manage representations on planning proposals.

## User journeys / Public consultation submissions

[P-01] Service commitment 1
  The adjacent analytics platform is excluded from scope, yet the appointed provider must redesign its data model and screens.

[P-02] Service commitment 2
  The staffing annex calls for night-shift training at each operating site, incorporated in the fixed fee.

[P-03] Service commitment 3
  Submit clarification questions and an evidence request before committing to scope. Proposals cannot rely on inspecting code.

[P-04] Service commitment 4
  Guarantee the migration scope while legacy source, database structure and sample records remain unavailable.

[P-05] Service commitment 5
  Digital delivery requires accessibility verification under EN 301 549 prior to sign-off.

[P-06] Service commitment 6
  All sites shall be live by week 10.

[P-07] Service commitment 7
  Identity infrastructure is outside scope, but the supplier shall replace the enterprise directory as part of delivery.

[P-08] Service commitment 8
  An external regulator accepts exclusively the incumbent nightly batch file, which remains a live dependency.

[P-09] Service commitment 9
  The integration inventory is incomplete and the incumbent owner cannot identify each individual active consumer.

[P-10] Service commitment 10
  Procure a capability to manage representations on planning proposals, currently supported by Java and PostgreSQL.

[P-11] Service commitment 11
  Interface boundary: submission windows; maintain continuity for public consultation submissions.

[P-12] Service commitment 12
  Operators can override eligibility decisions without recording who changed the decision or why.

[P-13] Service commitment 13
  For public consultation submissions, document and test the handling of response themes.

[P-14] Service commitment 14
  For public consultation submissions, document and test the handling of submission windows.

[P-15] Service commitment 15
  Provide extended support hours whenever required; coverage days and escalation response times are undefined.

[P-16] Service commitment 16
  For public consultation submissions, document and test the handling of redaction choices.

[P-17] Service commitment 17
  Processing personal data calls for individual authorisation, environment separation and operation logging.

[P-18] Service commitment 18
  Backups are copied daily, but the contract stipulates no restore test or recovery evidence.

[P-19] Service commitment 19
  The finance annex stipulates manual reconciliation of unmatched historic transactions prior to final payment.

[P-20] Service commitment 20
  Preserve the complete set of existing calculation outputs, encompassing undocumented rounding edge cases.


## Operational promises / response themes

[A-01] Service commitment 1
  The payment appendix quietly assigns monthly printed notice production and postal fulfilment to the delivery partner.

[A-02] Service commitment 2
  The mandatory discovery phase cannot start until the source owner grants access in week 20.

[A-03] Service commitment 3
  Guarantee fast response under realistic usage, in the absence of defining concurrency or a performance acceptance boundary.

[A-04] Service commitment 4
  The communications appendix stipulates certified translation of historic free-text records into three languages.

[A-05] Service commitment 5
  Recovery exercises must demonstrate a four-hour restoration target and a one-hour data-loss limit.

[A-06] Service commitment 6
  The proposed solution must export structured records using documented open formats, with an annual portability test.

[A-07] Service commitment 7
  Each release must pass regression tests in a dedicated non-production environment before approval.
