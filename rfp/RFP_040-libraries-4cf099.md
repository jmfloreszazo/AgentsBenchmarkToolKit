## Operational promises / reservation queues

[A-01] Service commitment 1
  The procurement terms offers no exit assistance, source escrow or usable data-export commitment if the supplier fails.

[A-02] Service commitment 2
  The field acceptance test calls for first-time login with no network connection.

[A-03] Service commitment 3
  Privileged override actions bypass the audit trail and require no documented justification.

[A-04] Service commitment 4
  Downstream statutory reporting still consumes the incumbent nightly fixed-width batch export.

[A-05] Service commitment 5
  The earliest approved access to the required source systems is week 18, after the rollout deadline.

[A-06] Service commitment 6
  Recovery exercises shall demonstrate a four-hour restoration target and a one-hour data-loss limit.

[A-07] Service commitment 7
  The operational system must export structured records using documented open formats, with an annual portability test.

[A-08] Service commitment 8
  Each release shall pass regression tests in a dedicated non-production environment before approval.
